# Design directions for miobirk.com, round 2

Round 1 was declined; its directions were invented aesthetics. These three
are derived from the reference research in `REFERENCES.md`: each takes one of
the three editorial modes observed in major publications and applies the
structural lessons of the peer survey. All three share that structure and
differ in temperament.

## Shared structure (from the peer survey, fixed across all three)

- The hero leads with her claim and the free book, never a CV or a feed.
  The primary action is "Hent bogen gratis".
- Three doors: Foredrag og undervisning (practitioners and institutions),
  Presse (a real press kit), and the writing itself.
- Writing is structured by register: Bøger, Fagfællebedømt, Formidling,
  Essay, Digt, presented as a table of contents with excerpts, not cards.
- Ikke Bare Køn is the named platform inside the site.
- Danish, sentence case, spaced en-dash, quotations verbatim in »...«.
- Open-source typefaces only; production self-hosts via @fontsource.
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum; body measure 60 to 75 characters.

## Retning 4: Tidsskriftet (the intellectual review)

Model: London Review of Books, The Paris Review. The site as a small,
serious review of one author's work. Serif authority, a contents-page
publication list with excerpts, one signature accent in the LRB tradition.

- **Type:** Newsreader (display and body; a text face made for reading on
  screens, with real italics and optical sizes) with Public Sans for
  navigation and metadata.
- **Palette:** warm white `#FBF9F4`, ink `#1A1817`, one signature deep red
  `#9E2B25` used the way the LRB uses its red: masthead, links, accents.
- **Layout grammar:** narrow reading column, hairline rules, the manifesto
  set as the opening paragraph of an essay; publications as a true table of
  contents (title, venue, year, one-line excerpt); the book presented in the
  Machado formula (cover, one framing line, free download first).
- **Risk:** reads conservative. The claim-led hero and the frankness of the
  three doors are what keep it hers.

## Retning 5: Formidleren (the humane Danish editorial)

Model: Zetland. Warm, direct, member-journalism Danish: reading times on
everything, conversational microcopy, a palette with actual warmth. The
direction that most serves practitioners and bi+ readers who are not
academics.

- **Type:** Literata (a book face built for long reading, warm and sturdy)
  with Instrument Sans for interface and metadata.
- **Palette:** butter `#FAF3E2`, warm ink `#26211A`, ochre accent `#9A5B00`,
  with a deep bottle green `#1F4B3F` for grounds and the footer.
- **Layout grammar:** modular but calm; every piece of writing carries an
  honest reading time ("6 min"); microcopy speaks directly ("Skriv til mig,
  jeg svarer gerne"); the book block states plainly that it is free and why.
- **Risk:** warmth can read soft. The register labels and full citations
  keep the scholarly spine visible.

## Retning 6: Profilen (the elegant profile magazine)

Model: The Gentlewoman, The White Review. The site as a beautifully set
profile of one person: modern grotesque, generous white, biographical
elegance, charm in the voice rather than ornament. Built around the credited
portrait the peer survey shows is the genre's status object.

- **Type:** Familjen Grotesk (a Scandinavian open-source grotesque with
  genuine character) for everything, with its italic doing the literary
  work; scale contrast replaces typeface contrast.
- **Palette:** soft white `#F7F6F2`, near-black `#191919`, one powder blue
  accent `#31547D`; portrait imagery in duotone until real photography
  exists (mocked as an art-directed frame with a photographer credit line,
  never stock).
- **Layout grammar:** centred masthead, oval portrait frame as the one
  curved element in an otherwise square page (The Gentlewoman's lozenge),
  profile-style standfirst ("Mio Tiger Birk undersøger..." in third person),
  publications as a clean index, doors as a modest, well-mannered list.
- **Risk:** without strong photography it must stand on type alone; the
  mockup deliberately tests that worst case.

## What to judge them on

Same test as before, plus one: whether each direction would survive beside
its model (the LRB, Zetland, The Gentlewoman) without embarrassment, and
whether it would beat emmaholten.com on seriousness and warmth at once.
