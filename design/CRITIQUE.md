# Critique round 3, 2026-07-10

The round-3 mockups (Overlappet, Natlæsning, Overstregning) were reviewed by
the same two agents as earlier rounds: an art and design critic and a
literary and editorial critic. Verdicts and applied fixes below; the diff of
the refinement commit is the full record.

## Verdicts

The critics converged. **Overlappet (retning 7)** should ship: it is the
only direction where the register triad functions as a legible system (a
coloured spine plus a genre word per publication), the purple underline
under "bare" is the one place colour carries the argument, and it survives
every audience in the brief. **Overstregning (retning 9)** is the best
fallback and the truest answer to the Soulellis frame, highlighting as a
queer act of reading, but its execution betrayed the concept (lost reading
measure, white-out marks reading as redaction, a three-colour hero that
pre-digests her prose). **Natlæsning (retning 8)** should not ship as the
identity: its tri-coloured hero was the rainbow-skin failure the research
was commissioned to avoid ("pink on the word Køn literally paints gender
pink"), its flag device was invisible on the night ground, and the nightlife
register sits uneasily against a violence-prevention book. Both critics
recommend keeping it as the dark scheme of Overlappet.

The literary critic's closing on the triad stands as the round's most
important sentence: only the purple is intellectually earned (the flag's
own overlap stripe pointed at a book about landing between); blue-research
and pink-poetry are convention that flirts with the very cliché her work
dismantles, and shipping the triad is Mio's call to make knowingly. The
system therefore ships in constrained form only: colours where genre words
anchor them, purple for the book, the flag quoted once.

## Applied fixes

All three: her own site's taxonomy now governs the labels (Forskningsartikel,
Essay, Artikel, Digt, Bog, her words, one axis); "Gratis" said once instead
of three times, with the funder line corrected to "Udgivet med støtte fra
Offerfonden. Bogen er gratis, og du skal hverken oprette dig eller oplyse
din e-mailadresse."; the title en-dash; "min ph.d. ... (2019)"; the
signature no longer wears "ph.d."; the duplicate nav targets merged to
"Foredrag og presse"; optical-size pins removed everywhere except true
display so Fraunces's engineered opsz axis does its work; display
line-heights declared; hovers stay inside their element's register.

Overlappet: the full-bleed flag banner became a 7rem quotation above the
book block; the hero kicker no longer duplicates the platform name above
the h1; the three doors lost their one-of-each colours (neutral ink rules);
the register key is a real exposed list, its middle label plainened from
"Det, der lander imellem" to "Formidling og essays".

Natlæsning: the hero is cream with a single purple italic "i bevægelse", no
pink "Køn", no coloured full stop; the flag rule was dropped (the ground
cannot carry canonical #0038A8); the cover is now the sole cream object in
the section; a lightened rosa variant carries small labels on night; body
weight raised to 450 for dark-ground rendering.

Overstregning: one mark only, pink over "ikke bare om køn", handing pink
the argument itself; the white-out solid marks deleted; hero and Om
returned to the 44rem reading measure; the cover no longer annotates its
own title and is now a real ink object; the door chips share one tint.

## Open questions for Ole and Mio

1. Whether the constrained triad ships at all: the purple-as-overlap is
   earned; blue and pink are convention. Mio must own the choice.
2. Whether Natlæsning becomes Overlappet's prefers-color-scheme: dark.
3. Whether Overstregning's one-mark hero should replace Overlappet's hero
   (the critics note it is arguably the stronger opening if she prefers
   leading with the manifesto over the name).
4. Offerfonden's required attribution formula must be checked against the
   grant terms before the book page ships.
