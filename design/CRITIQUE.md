# Critique round 2, 2026-07-09

The round-2 mockups were reviewed by the same two independent critique
agents as round 1: an art and design critic and a literary and editorial
critic. This records their verdicts and the applied refinements; the diff of
the refinement commit is the full record.

## Verdicts

The critics converged this round. **Formidleren (retning 5)** was the art
critic's strongest-as-is: the only direction that already clears both halves
of the test, seriousness and warmth at once, and the round's best inventions
are its honesty devices. The literary critic agreed it best serves the book
and its practitioner and bi+ audiences, but found its signature device,
invented reading times, inadmissible until repaired, and noted its voice is
Zetland's rather than Mio's. **Tidsskriftet (retning 4)** was the art
critic's most-potential and the literary critic's outright recommendation:
the only direction where her thesis survives verbatim with "derfor", where a
peer-reviewed article and a poem sit in one dignified system, and the mode
that matches an essayist-poet-researcher specifically; its conservatism is
curable by importing Formidleren's candour. **Profilen (retning 6)** was
judged the thin costume of the round by the art critic (it would survive
beside the Squarespace cohort, not beside The Gentlewoman) and the most
elegant but least-her by the literary critic; conditionally curable with a
real credited portrait, structural scale, and a voice decision.

## Applied fixes

Tidsskriftet: red rationed to masthead, links and the one filled button
(genre labels went grey); the publication list gained a real margin rail so
the page no longer abandons its right half; the book section sits on a
shifted ground so the cover reads as an object; "Tre indgange" renamed
"Foredrag, presse og læsning"; the constructed self-quotation in Om lost its
guillemets and became plain prose; the invented "København" left the
masthead; "Honorar efter aftale" added to the foredrag door; Formidleren's
no-signup line imported into the book block.

Formidleren: reading times survive only on freely readable formidling
pieces, marked "ca.", and were removed from the poem and the paywalled
journal articles; "Bogen er gratis, fordi projektet bag den er støttet af
Offerfonden"; the doors now speak with one voice from the visitor's seat
("Vil du booke et foredrag?") and the ambiguous "Skriv om et foredrag"
became "Skriv til mig om et foredrag"; the unverified "17" left the
publications link; "Så er du kommet til det rigtige sted"; "Mere at læse"
replaced the breezy "Læs mere af mig"; the header went light so the green
is rationed to the book panel and footer; the hero headline balances its
rag; the book actions no longer collide on mobile.

Profilen: the pill button squared, restoring the oval's monopoly on
curvature; the gradient-blob placeholder became a flat keylined oval that
states its own absence; sections left-set with real display scale while the
masthead keeps the one centred axis; the index gained the one-line glosses
and a proper mobile stack; the italic charm device cut back to a single
use; the hero paragraph restored the thesis's reasoning sentence;
"I tillæg" corrected to "Derudover"; first person kept deliberately, the
profile conceit traded for an elegant-grotesque editorial register.

Shared: the Viagra gloss corrected from a causal claim to what the article
is ("Om de betydninger af maskulinitet, Viagra sætter i spil i
nyhedsmedierne").

## Standing recommendation from the critics

Both point to the same composite if a single direction must win:
Tidsskriftet's spine (the review-of-one-author frame, the contents-page
publication list, the verbatim thesis) with Formidleren's candour (the
no-signup line, ca.-marked reading times on freely readable pieces, the
direct-address doors). The choice remains Ole's and Mio's.
