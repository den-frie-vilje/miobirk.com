# Research: Mio Birk and the existing miobirk.com

Recorded 2026-07-09 as the factual basis for the redesign directions in
`DESIGN-DIRECTIONS.md`. Sources: the live site at miobirk.com (Squarespace),
its linked profiles, and public publication records.

## Who Mio is

Mio Tiger Birk, ph.d., is a Danish gender researcher, writer, and poet. PhD in
social sciences from Aarhus University (2019). She works across registers that
few people combine: peer-reviewed research (Sexualities, Somatechnics,
Mediekultur, International Journal of Drug Policy, Nordic Studies on Alcohol
and Drugs), essays, popular science (Videnskab.dk), reports for the Nordic
Council of Ministers, magazine writing, and published poetry (Slagtryk,
Friktion). She is queer, and her work is explicitly situated in queer and
intersectional feminist scholarship.

Her platform is named **Ikke Bare Køn** (Not Just Gender). Its thesis, quoted
from the site, is the intellectual centre of the identity:

> Køn er altid i bevægelse. For at forstå kønnets betydning må vi tage højde
> for alt det, køn væves sammen med. Når noget handler om køn, handler det
> derfor ikke bare om køn.

Gender is always in motion; when something is about gender, it is never only
about gender. Any design that fixes, freezes, or flattens is off-brief.

## Current work

**Tvivlen Skygger for Trygheden: Når biseksuelle lander mellem to stole**
(Ikke Bare Køn, 2026). A free book on the living conditions of bi+ people in
Denmark, funded by Offerfonden, aimed at practitioners who work with
discrimination and violence prevention, but written for everyone. Distributed
as a free read/download. This is the site's primary call to action. "Mellem to
stole" (between two chairs) is her recurring figure: bisexual people falling
between categories, and (in her ADHD magazine article) the ambivalence of
partial belonging generally.

## The existing site

- Squarespace template, Danish, three pages: Mellem To Stole, Udgivelser,
  Kontakt, plus a home page with the manifesto and a "Læs mere" link.
- Type: Libre Baskerville (display) and Almarai (body), the template's stock
  pairing. A commerce cart icon ("0") appears in the header although nothing
  is for sale.
- Imagery: an Unsplash stock photograph in the hero and screenshots of the
  book PDF used as images, both signs of a template pressed into service.
- The publication list is a flat run of paragraphs with no typographic
  hierarchy between a peer-reviewed article, a poem, and a blog post.
- Social: LinkedIn and Bluesky (@ikkebarekon.bsky.social). Contact is
  kontakt(a)miobirk.com, obfuscated by hand.

What works and must be kept: the name Ikke Bare Køn, the manifesto text, the
"mellem to stole" figure, the breadth of the publication list, Danish as the
primary language, and the direct mailto contact.

What fails and motivates the rebuild: template anonymity, stock photography,
no typographic voice, no hierarchy in the publications, screenshots instead of
designed objects, the stray cart icon.

## Audience

1. Practitioners and institutions (violence and discrimination prevention,
   education, unions, municipalities) who download the book and might book
   her to speak or teach.
2. Journalists and editors looking for a gender researcher who can write.
3. Peers in gender studies and queer communities, including bi+ readers whom
   the book addresses directly.

The site must read as credible scholarship and as living, queer, literary
writing at the same time. That doubleness is the design problem.
